TACH CAU HINH MAY A (DEV) VA MAY B (CAI DAT)
=============================================

MAY A - DUNG APP HANG NGAY (Visual Studio / F5)
-----------------------------------------------
- appsettings.json = http://localhost:5191/api/
- KHONG tao file server.url trong thu muc project (neu co thi xoa):
    .\scripts\clean-dev-server-url.ps1
- Chi can: SQL + WebApplication3 (port 5191). KHONG can ngrok de ban hang tren A.

MAY A - CHUAN BI GOI CAI CHO MAY KHAC
--------------------------------------
1. Bat ngrok:  ngrok http 5191
2. Luu URL cho installer:
     .\scripts\update-ngrok-url.ps1 -ForInstaller
   (ghi vao installer\remote-server.url)
3. Build:
     .\build-setup.ps1 -Force
4. Copy thu muc dist\ShopBanHang-Installer sang may B.

MAY B
-----
- Chay setup.exe, chon URL ngrok (hoac discovery).
- Goi cai da co san server.url (ngrok), khong dung localhost.

KHI NGROK DOI URL
-----------------
- May A dev: khong lam gi (van localhost).
- Goi cai may B: update-ngrok-url.ps1 -ForInstaller -> build-setup.ps1 -Force
  HOAC tren may B: Cau hinh server trong app.
